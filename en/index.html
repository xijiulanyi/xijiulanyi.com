Based on real procurement and trade, we provide trading entities, applicable export paths and cross-border account service arrangements, connecting goods payment collection, overseas operating expenses and cross-border settlement. Cross-border investment, financing and M&A services

We provide Kazakhstan company registration, amendments, account opening assistance, bookkeeping, tax filing, visa and product certification support, connecting company setup with ongoing operations.

Direct KZT Collection Supported

Through a strategic partnership, Shumei Information’s licensed Hong Kong entity and licensed institutions in Kazakhstan have established a two-way cross-border payment channel for businesses. It supports the collection of payments directly in Kazakhstani tenge (KZT) for settlement to corporate accounts in mainland China or Hong Kong, as well as payments from businesses in mainland China and Hong Kong to businesses in Kazakhstan.

Xijiu Lanyi coordinates the solution, account arrangements and system integration around each client’s business, reducing duplicate processing, manual reconciliation and repeated document exchanges.

About the logo: This flying bird has two sources of inspiration. One is the form of the character “West” in oracle-bone script; the other is the mythical black bird of the Shang dynasty totem, from the famous line “Heaven commanded the black bird to descend and give birth to Shang.” We fused the form of the oracle-bone “West” with the spirit of the bird to create the “Xijiu Cross-Border” logo, which carries the company’s cultural ethos. Two layers of meaning: going west stands for perseverance, exploration and pioneering advance; the colorful bird returning east is a brilliant sight, symbolizing complete achievement and destiny fulfilled.
